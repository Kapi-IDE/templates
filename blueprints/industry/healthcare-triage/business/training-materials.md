## Training Program Overview

### Learning Objectives
By the completion of this training program, participants will be able to:
- Efficiently navigate and utilize the Healthcare Triage System
- Integrate AI-powered triage into existing clinical workflows
- Interpret and act upon AI recommendations appropriately
- Maintain HIPAA compliance and patient safety standards
- Troubleshoot common system issues and know when to escalate
- Optimize patient care through effective use of system features

### Training Methodology
- **Blended Learning**: Combination of online modules, hands-on practice, and in-person sessions
- **Competency-Based**: Progress measured by demonstrated skills rather than time
- **Microlearning**: Bite-sized lessons for busy healthcare professionals
- **Scenario-Based**: Real-world case studies and simulation exercises
- **Continuous Reinforcement**: Ongoing support and refresher training

### Duration and Schedule
- **Initial Certification**: 16 hours over 2 weeks
- **Role-Specific Modules**: 4-8 additional hours
- **Hands-On Practice**: 8 hours supervised practice
- **Competency Assessment**: 2 hours testing and evaluation
- **Annual Recertification**: 4 hours yearly

## Module 3: AI-Assisted Triage

### Learning Objectives
- Understand AI recommendation algorithms and confidence scoring
- Appropriately interpret and act on AI assessments
- Recognize when to override AI recommendations
- Maintain clinical judgment while leveraging AI support

### Content Outline

#### 3.1 Understanding AI Recommendations (90 minutes)
**Topics Covered**:
- How AI analyzes patient data
- Confidence scoring interpretation
- Recommendation categories and priorities
- Limitation awareness and appropriate use

**AI Algorithm Explanation**:
```
Input Factors Analyzed:
- Vital signs and trends
- Symptom combinations and severity
- Medical history and risk factors
- Age, gender, and demographic factors
- Medication interactions and contraindications
- Recent healthcare utilization patterns

Output Generated:
- Triage level recommendation (CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)
- Confidence percentage (0-100%)
- Risk factors identified
- Suggested interventions
- Red flag warnings
- Follow-up recommendations
```

#### 3.2 Clinical Decision Making with AI Support (120 minutes)
**Topics Covered**:
- Integrating AI insights with clinical judgment
- When to trust vs. question AI recommendations
- Documentation of clinical reasoning
- Override protocols and justification

**Decision Framework**:
1. **Review AI Assessment**
   - Analyze triage level recommendation
   - Evaluate confidence score
   - Consider identified risk factors

2. **Apply Clinical Judgment**
   - Consider patient appearance and behavior
   - Factor in clinical experience and intuition
   - Assess social and environmental factors

3. **Make Final Decision**
   - Agree with AI recommendation
   - Override with clinical justification
   - Request additional evaluation if uncertain

**Case Studies for Practice**:
```
Case 1: High Confidence, Clear Recommendation
- 65-year-old male, chest pain, diaphoresis
- AI: CRITICAL priority, 95% confidence
- Decision: Agree and activate emergency protocols

Case 2: Low Confidence, Unclear Presentation
- 35-year-old female, vague abdominal pain
- AI: MEDIUM priority, 60% confidence
- Decision: Consider override based on clinical assessment

Case 3: Conflicting Clinical Impression
- 22-year-old athlete, shortness of breath
- AI: LOW priority, 85% confidence
- Clinical concern for pulmonary embolism
- Decision: Override to HIGH priority with justification
```

#### 3.3 Managing Complex and Edge Cases (75 minutes)
**Topics Covered**:
- Patients with multiple comorbidities
- Pediatric and geriatric considerations
- Mental health and substance use factors
- Social determinants impact on care

**Special Populations Guidelines**:
- **Pediatric Patients**: Age-specific normal values and assessment modifications
- **Elderly Patients**: Atypical presentations and medication considerations
- **Mental Health**: Risk assessment and safety planning
- **Language Barriers**: Cultural competency and interpreter services

#### 3.4 Quality Assurance and Feedback (60 minutes)
**Topics Covered**:
- Monitoring AI accuracy and performance
- Providing feedback for system improvement
- Participating in quality reviews
- Continuous learning and adaptation

### Module 3 Assessment
- **AI Interpretation Test**: Analyze 10 AI recommendations and provide clinical reasoning
- **Case Study Analysis**: Complete complex multi-factor patient scenarios
- **Override Justification**: Document appropriate clinical override decisions

## Certification Program

### Initial Certification Requirements
1. **Complete All Required Modules**: Pass all assessments with minimum scores
2. **Hands-On Competency**: Demonstrate skills with real patients under supervision
3. **Final Examination**: Comprehensive 100-question exam (90% passing score)
4. **Peer Review**: Positive evaluation from supervisor or mentor
5. **Quality Metrics**: Meet performance standards during probationary period

### Certification Levels

#### Level 1: Basic User Certification
- **Requirements**: Modules 1-3, basic competency demonstration
- **Privileges**: Standard system access, patient registration and assessment
- **Renewal**: Annual recertification required

#### Level 2: Advanced User Certification
- **Requirements**: All modules, advanced competency demonstration
- **Privileges**: Advanced features, report generation, quality review participation
- **Renewal**: Biennial recertification with continuing education

#### Level 3: Super User Certification
- **Requirements**: Advanced certification plus training others, quality improvement participation
- **Privileges**: System configuration, user training, quality assurance leadership
- **Renewal**: Triennial recertification with leadership development

### Continuing Education Requirements
- **Annual Training**: 8 hours minimum continuing education
- **Quality Participation**: Active involvement in quality improvement initiatives
- **Peer Learning**: Participation in case reviews and best practice sharing
- **Technology Updates**: Training on new features and system updates
